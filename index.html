For when you laugh at the worst time

Calm inside, honest outside.

People can't see what you feel. They only see your face. Here's what to do in the next five seconds.

1

Name it in your head

Putting a feeling into words calms the alarm part of your brain. UCLA, 2007 (30 people).

2

Breathe: two in, one long out

Ready
in · in · out

Five minutes a day of this beat meditation for mood. Stanford, 2023 (108 people).

3

If you still laugh, say it

Don't hide it behind your hand. Hiding looks guilty. One honest sentence turns “rude” into “human.”

The mistake everyone makes

Don't fight it. Rename it.

“Calm down.”
