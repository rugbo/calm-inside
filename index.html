Step one

Name it.

Say it in your head, not out loud:

“This is stress. Not funny.”

UCLA · 2007Naming a feeling calms your brain's alarm. 30 people tested.
02

Step two

Breathe.

Two quick breaths in. One long breath out. Try it now.

Ready?
in · in · out

03

Step three

Say it.

Still laughing? Don't hide it. Own it.

Sorry, I laugh when I'm nervous.

Hiding looks guilty. Honest looks human.

Stop saying “calm down.”
